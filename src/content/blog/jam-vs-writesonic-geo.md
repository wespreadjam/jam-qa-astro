## What does each tool actually do?

**Writesonic** started as an AI writing tool. A competitor's review says it was [founded in 2021 and added AI visibility tools in mid-2025](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/writesonic-review-what-its-ai-visibility-suite-gets-wrong). Today its pricing page describes a platform with AI visibility tracking, an Article Writer, site audits and agentic workflows. Its [Action Center](https://writesonic.com/ai-visibility-action-center) ranks opportunities by impact, confidence and effort across three areas: content, external citations and technical issues. It also checks whether AI crawlers such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot are blocked.

**Jam** monitors AI visibility and then acts on it. Its Visibility Score is the [share of tracked prompts where you are cited](https://spreadjam.com/), and "Find Your Gaps" shows where you are not cited. Jam then writes articles and opens pull requests for them. It also covers the outbound side: lead enrichment, mailbox warm-up and a single inbox for replies.

## Which AI engines do Jam and Writesonic track?

Jam tracks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot on [every plan](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing).

Writesonic's self-serve plans track ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. Perplexity, Google AI Mode and Copilot are not in that list. Writesonic's Enterprise plan lists "All 9 AI platforms" without naming them, per its [pricing page](https://writesonic.com/pricing). Its own pages disagree on the count: a [Writesonic comparison page](https://writesonic.com/compare/writesonic-vs-ahrefs) says it tracks 8 engines daily. A competitor, [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/writesonic-review-what-its-ai-visibility-suite-gets-wrong), lists eight Enterprise engines including Claude, Copilot and Grok. If Perplexity or AI Mode matter to your buyers, Jam covers them without an Enterprise contract.

## How much do Jam and Writesonic GEO cost?

Writesonic publishes four plans, all billed annually. Source for every row: [Writesonic pricing](https://writesonic.com/pricing).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Basic | $199 ([pricing](https://writesonic.com/pricing)) | 100 | 25 | 2 | 1 |
| Growth | $399 ([pricing](https://writesonic.com/pricing)) | 200 | 50 | 3 | 2 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom |

The page does not show monthly-billed prices. Writesonic's Ahrefs comparison says "From $99/mo", which differs from the $79 Starter price on the pricing page. Older competitor reviews quote different plan names and prices ($249 and $499), so use the pricing page.

Jam's [pricing page](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing) says "Make your own plan" and shows no dollar amounts. Every plan gets the full product, and all work draws from one credit balance. An article costs about 150 credits, and the page says 2,000 credits covers about 13 articles. Top-ups never expire. A competitor, [Runlo](https://www.tryrunlo.com/compare/spreadjam-alternative), reports Jam plans at $39 per month (10,000 credits) and $79 per seat. Those figures are not on Jam's own page, so treat them as a third party's report.

The practical difference is predictability. Writesonic's fixed tiers cap prompts and articles. Jam's credits scale with the work you request, so cost follows usage.

## Does Writesonic GEO or Jam help you fix visibility gaps?

Both do, in different ways.

- **Writesonic:** The Action Center suggests content to write, pages to refresh (it cites "15+ GEO levers" such as FAQs, comparison tables and fresh data), and sites that cite competitors. On the pricing page, [Growth includes an Action Center trial of 5 off-page and 5 on-page actions per month](https://writesonic.com/pricing). The full Action Center is on Enterprise.
- **Jam:** Articles are researched, written and shipped as pull requests. Jam can create a [PR with blog updates](https://spreadjam.com/) and post it to Slack for your approval. A GitHub-based workflow suits sites that live in a repository.

On content quality, a hands-on review from [AlloyPress](https://alloypress.com/reviews/writesonic) found Writesonic allows a maximum of 10 competitors per project, and Profound, a rival vendor, [rated Writesonic's draft quality low](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/writesonic-review-what-its-ai-visibility-suite-gets-wrong). Treat both as opinions and test drafts from either tool on your own topics.

## Can either tool do outreach?

Outreach is where the two differ most. Jam sends cold email: it enriches leads, [warms up mailboxes automatically and gives you one inbox](https://spreadjam.com/) for replies. Writesonic's outreach is aimed at earning citations. The Action Center finds sites that cite competitors and [drafts outreach in-platform](https://writesonic.com/ai-visibility-action-center). The Outreach Agent is listed as [Enterprise only](https://writesonic.com/pricing).

## Where does each tool fall short?

**Jam**

- The [pricing page](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing) shows no dollar prices, plan names or credit allowances per plan.
- The pricing and home pages list no sentiment analysis and no security certifications.
- It is not an SEO suite. It has a Google Search Console integration and keyword, SERP and traffic analysis, but the pricing page shows no site audit tool.

**Writesonic**

- The three self-serve plans track three engines. Wider coverage and the full Action Center sit on Enterprise ([pricing](https://writesonic.com/pricing)).
- Per the pricing page, the Starter and Basic plans have no Action Center. Growth has a limited trial of it.
- Articles are capped per plan, with an add-on of 20 articles for $100 per month ([pricing](https://writesonic.com/pricing)).

## Who should pick Jam, and who should pick Writesonic?

**Pick Jam if:**

- You are a founder or small growth team and want visibility tracking, content and outbound in one tool.
- You need Perplexity, Google AI Mode and Copilot tracked from the first plan.
- Your site is in a Git repository and you want articles delivered as pull requests.
- You prefer paying for work done over a fixed seat and article tier.

**Pick Writesonic if:**

- You want a fixed monthly price and a clear plan ladder.
- You need sentiment analysis, site audits and an article writer in one subscription.
- You need documented enterprise security. [SSO/SAML, SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA and GDPR](https://writesonic.com/pricing) are listed for Enterprise.
- Your buyers mainly use ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews.

## FAQ

**Is Writesonic a GEO tool or an AI writer?**
Both. Its [pricing page](https://writesonic.com/pricing) leads with AI search visibility tracking and includes an Article Writer and site audits on every plan.

**Does Jam track more engines than Writesonic?**
On self-serve plans, yes: six on [Jam](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing), three on [Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/pricing). Writesonic Enterprise lists nine platforms without naming them.

**How often do they scan?**
Writesonic tracks daily on all plans. Jam scans weekly, every other day or daily, depending on the plan ([Jam pricing](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing)).

**Do both offer a free start?**
Jam's pricing page says "Start free." Writesonic offers a free trial on self-serve plans, and its [Ahrefs comparison](https://writesonic.com/compare/writesonic-vs-ahrefs) states a 7-day trial with no card. Its pricing page does not state the length.
