**Short answer:** most B2B SaaS founders should start with a tool that tracks 50 to 100 buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot, and that costs under $300 a month. At published prices, that points to [Otterly](https://otterly.ai/pricing) (from $29/mo), [Peec AI](https://peec.ai/pricing) (from $95/mo) or [Searchable](https://www.searchable.com/pricing) (from $125/mo). If you already pay for Semrush or Ahrefs, add their AI module first. If you want the tool to also publish content that earns citations, look at Searchable, AthenaHQ or [Jam](https://spreadjam.com/pricing).

## Which AI search visibility tool should a B2B SaaS founder use in 2026?

Pick by what you need to do next, not by feature count.

- **You need to see whether you are cited at all:** a monitoring tool with 15 to 50 prompts is enough. [Otterly Lite](https://otterly.ai/pricing) (15 prompts) and [Peec Starter](https://peec.ai/pricing) (50 prompts) fit.
- **You already know you are missing and want to fix it:** you need citation sources, content recommendations or publishing. Searchable, AthenaHQ and Jam bundle that work.
- **You already run an SEO suite:** the Semrush and Ahrefs modules put AI tracking next to data you already use.
- **You sell to enterprises and need procurement features:** Profound, Peec Enterprise and AthenaHQ Enterprise list SSO and custom terms.

## How to choose

1. **Count your prompts.** A B2B SaaS company usually tracks category prompts ("best X for Y"), competitor comparisons and use-case questions. Otterly's own guidance is that the number depends on [markets, languages and products](https://otterly.ai/pricing). Start with 50 to 100 and compare tools on cost per prompt.
2. **Check which engines are included, not just listed.** Plans differ on what is in the base price. Otterly sells AI Mode, Gemini and Claude as [add-ons](https://otterly.ai/pricing). Peec Starter to Advanced include [3 active models](https://peec.ai/pricing). Searchable includes [three models](https://www.searchable.com/pricing). Ahrefs Brand Radar covers [six engines in its index](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar).
3. **Decide between monitoring and action.** Monitoring tools report mentions and citations. Action tools add content, audits or robots.txt and llms.txt management. Searchable's plans include [20 to 200 articles a month](https://www.searchable.com/pricing), and AthenaHQ lists [llms.txt management and content optimization agents](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing).
4. **Read the pricing model.** Per-domain (Semrush), per-prompt (Otterly, Peec, Searchable), per-check (Ahrefs) and per-credit (AthenaHQ, Jam) models scale differently as you add prompts.
5. **Test answer stability.** AI answers change between runs. Use a trial, or a plan you can cancel monthly, and rerun the same 20 prompts to see how stable the numbers are before committing a year. Otterly, for example, says [all subscriptions are monthly and can be cancelled anytime](https://otterly.ai/pricing).

## Tool by tool

### Otterly.AI

Lite is $29/mo for 15 prompts, Standard $189/mo for 100 and Premium $489/mo for 400, with annual billing [15% cheaper](https://otterly.ai/pricing). Standard and Premium add API and MCP access and a Data Studio connector. Extra prompts cost $99 per 100 on Standard and Premium, and Lite cannot add prompts. It is the cheapest published way to start.

### Peec AI

Starter is $95/mo (50 prompts), Pro $245/mo (150) and Advanced $495/mo (350), with unlimited users on every plan and SOC 2 included ([Peec pricing](https://peec.ai/pricing)). Enterprise is custom and lists up to 13 models. Peec's page lists Perplexity under Enterprise engines but also in its add-on model list, so confirm Perplexity access when you sign up if it matters to you.

### Searchable

Pro is $125/mo with 100 prompts per model, 20 articles a month and 200 site audits; Scale is $400/mo with 500 prompts and 80 articles; Enterprise starts at $999/mo ([Searchable pricing](https://www.searchable.com/pricing)). Pro and Scale show a 14-day free try. Looker Studio and white-label reports start at Scale.

### Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit

The standalone toolkit is $99/mo per domain with 25 tracked prompts; 50 more prompts cost $60/mo and each extra domain $99/mo ([Semrush help](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit)). Semrush One bundles it with SEO tools at 50, 100 or 200 prompts. A [competitor-run review](https://www.honeyb.ai/blog/semrush-ai-visibility-toolkit) puts those plans at $199, $299 and $549 a month. Semrush's own [pricing page](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/) shows no dollar amounts and lists unlimited prompts on Advanced, which disagrees with the 200 on the help page. Semrush pages name different engines in different places, so check coverage for the engines you care about.

### Ahrefs Brand Radar

Prompt tracking is [free for every paid Ahrefs Lite plan and above](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar), with a daily custom prompt quota of 5 on Lite, 10 on Standard and 20 on Advanced. Larger [custom prompt packages](https://ahrefs.com/pricing) cost $50/mo (Basic), $100/mo (Growth) and $250/mo (Scale). The AI Visibility Index covers 538 million prompts with no setup, which helps with competitor research before you track anything.

### AthenaHQ

Starter is $295/mo with 3,600 credits, where one credit is one AI response, plus unlimited topics, prompts and competitors ([Athena pricing](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing)). API access and extra credits are paid add-ons with unpublished prices. Enterprise adds SAML SSO, audit logs and a 2-hour SLA. The page also lists an Essential tier with no price.

### Profound

Profound's [pricing page](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) shows a free Trial (50 prompts daily for 7 days) and a custom Enterprise plan with up to 9 answer engines, SOC 2 and SSO. IndustryLens reports that Profound [removed its $99 Starter tier in October 2026](https://industry-lens.com/intelligence/ai-search), so older roundups that list $99 are out of date. Plan for a sales conversation.

## Where Jam fits

Jam tracks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot on every plan, with 50 to 1,000 tracked prompts and scans weekly, every other day or daily ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)). Its Visibility Score is the [share of tracked prompts where you are cited](https://spreadjam.com/), and it includes competitor gap analysis and a Google Search Console integration.

What sets Jam apart is that it does the follow-up work. Articles are researched, written and shipped as pull requests, and the [homepage](https://spreadjam.com/) describes Slack and GitHub approvals, a shared inbox, lead enrichment and mailbox warm-up for outreach. That suits a founder with no content team who wants one tool for tracking, publishing and outreach.

Pricing is credit-based: an article costs about 150 credits, a deep research report about 50 and a chat task 10 to 40, and "2,000 credits covers about 13 articles" ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)). Jam's pricing page shows no dollar price per plan or per credit, so you cannot compare Jam's cost with the tools above from the page alone. Neither the pricing page nor the homepage explains how Visibility Score prompts are chosen, and the integrations they list beyond Google Search Console are Gmail, Slack and GitHub. If you need BI dashboards for a large team or SSO, the enterprise tools above publish more on those points.

## FAQ

**How much do AI search visibility tools cost?**
Published entry prices run from [$29/mo (Otterly Lite)](https://otterly.ai/pricing) to [$295/mo (AthenaHQ Starter)](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing). [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) and [Evertune](https://www.evertune.ai/pricing) show no paid dollar prices on their pricing pages.

**How many prompts does a B2B SaaS company need?**
Start with 50 to 100 prompts across category, comparison and use-case questions, then grow once you see which ones move. Tools price prompts differently, so compare cost per prompt.

**Do I need a separate tool if I already use Semrush or Ahrefs?**
Not to start. Both include AI tracking, but with caps: Semrush's [standalone toolkit tracks 25 prompts](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit), and [Ahrefs Lite includes 5 daily custom prompts](https://ahrefs.com/brand-radar).

**Is there a free way to test?**
[Searchable](https://www.searchable.com/pricing) shows a 14-day free try, [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) a 7-day Trial, and [Otterly](https://otterly.ai/pricing) and [Peec](https://peec.ai/pricing) list free trials without a stated length. [Semrush's AI toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit) has no trial.
