---
title: How much should freelancers set aside for taxes?
description: Rules of thumb for US, UK and EU freelancers, with examples.
pubDate: 2026-10-01
---

A common rule is to set aside 25 to 30 percent of every payment in the US, 20 to 30 percent in the UK
and 30 to 40 percent in much of the EU, depending on income and deductions.

| Region | Typical set-aside |
|---|---|
| US | 25-30% |
| UK | 20-30% |
| Germany | 30-40% |

Talk to an accountant for your exact rate.
