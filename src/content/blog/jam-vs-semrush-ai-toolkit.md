## How does pricing compare?

Semrush charges a flat monthly price. The toolkit costs $99 per month and includes 1 project, 1 Brand Performance domain and 25 Prompt Tracking prompts. An extra domain costs $99 per month, 50 more prompts cost $60 per month, and each additional user needs another $99 license ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-toolkit)).

Semrush also sells the toolkit inside Semrush One bundles. Per Semrush, Starter includes 50 prompts, Pro+ includes 100 and Advanced includes 200 ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-toolkit)). Semrush's own pricing page lists Advanced as "Unlimited prompts to track daily" and shows no dollar amounts ([Semrush pricing](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/)). A competitor review lists the bundles at $199, $299 and $549 per month on monthly billing ([Honeyb](https://www.honeyb.ai/blog/semrush-ai-visibility-toolkit)). Another competitor review says the toolkit is billed on top of an existing Semrush subscription ([Rankability](https://www.rankability.com/blog/semrush-ai-toolkit-review)), while Semrush's help article lists $99 as the toolkit price. Confirm which applies to your account before you budget.

Jam uses one credit balance for all work. An article costs about 150 credits, a deep research report about 50, and a chat task about 10 to 40. Per Jam, 2,000 credits covers about 13 articles, top-ups never expire, and you can change plans at any time ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)). The pricing page shows no dollar prices and does not say what the free tier contains, so you cannot calculate a monthly cost from the page alone.

## Which AI engines does each tool track?

Jam names six engines on its pricing page: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot. Every plan gets the full product ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).

Semrush's pages disagree with each other. Its solutions page names ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, SearchGPT, Google AI Mode and Google AI Overviews ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/solutions/ai-visibility/)). Its help article names Google AI Mode and ChatGPT for Prompt Tracking ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-toolkit)). A competitor review that compared six Semrush pages found the same inconsistency ([Honeyb](https://www.honeyb.ai/blog/semrush-ai-visibility-toolkit)). Microsoft Copilot appears in neither the solutions page nor the help article.

## How many prompts can you track, and how often is data updated?

Jam supports 50 to 1,000 tracked prompts on standard plans and scans weekly, every other day or daily. Above 1,000 prompts you go to Enterprise ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).

Semrush Prompt Tracking updates daily and Brand Performance reports update weekly. Prompt Tracking covers 220+ countries and territories ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-toolkit)). At the base price you track 25 prompts. Semrush's toolkit also includes research reports, with 1,000 daily queries in Prompt Research and 300 daily queries in AI Analysis reports ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-toolkit)). Jam does not describe a comparable prompt research database.

## What happens after you find a gap?

This is the main difference. Semrush's toolkit measures and recommends. Its AI Search Optimizer analyzes existing content and gives prioritized recommendations, and its Site Audit flags AI search blockers ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/solutions/ai-visibility/), [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-toolkit)).

Jam goes on to do the work. Its dashboard shows where you are not cited, and its agents research and write articles and ship them as pull requests. You can approve drafts and review PRs from Slack and GitHub ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing), [Jam](https://spreadjam.com/)). For outreach, Jam offers one inbox for every account, lead enrichment, auto warm-up and custom automations, and it surfaces warm contacts already in your orbit ([Jam](https://spreadjam.com/)).

## Where Semrush is stronger

- **SEO suite.** The toolkit sits beside Semrush's SEO tools, with Site Audit, Position Tracking and keyword tracking in the same account ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/solutions/ai-visibility/), [Semrush pricing](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/)).
- **Reporting.** Semrush lists white-label reporting, shareable exports, automated email reports and competitor benchmarks ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/solutions/ai-visibility/)).
- **Published limits and methodology.** Semrush documents its score calculation, refresh rates, export limits and regional coverage ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-toolkit)). Jam's pages do not explain its Visibility Score method or publish dollar prices.
- **Track record.** Semrush has thousands of reviews across G2, Capterra and TrustRadius for the platform overall ([Honeyb](https://www.honeyb.ai/blog/semrush-ai-visibility-toolkit)).

## Who should pick which?

**Pick Semrush AI Toolkit if** you already run SEO in Semrush, you are an agency that needs white-label reports, or you want a fixed $99 per month price and daily tracking of a small prompt set ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-toolkit)).

**Pick Jam if** you are a founder or growth team that wants to track more prompts across six engines including Copilot, and you want articles shipped as pull requests plus outreach from the same product ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing), [Jam](https://spreadjam.com/)).

**Use both if** Semrush is your SEO source of record and you want Jam to turn gaps into published content.

## FAQ

### How much does the Semrush AI Toolkit cost?

The base toolkit is $99 per month with 25 tracked prompts. Extra prompts are $60 per month per 50, and extra domains or users are $99 each ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-toolkit)).

### Does the Semrush AI Toolkit have a free trial?

1. Semrush's help article says the toolkit has no free trial ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-toolkit)). Semrush's solutions page offers 7-day access to its marketing toolkits ([Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/solutions/ai-visibility/)).

### Which engines does Jam track?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).

### How does Jam charge?

Through work credits. You are charged for the work performed, and top-ups never expire ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).

### Can Jam publish content?

Yes. Jam researches and writes articles and ships them as pull requests ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).
