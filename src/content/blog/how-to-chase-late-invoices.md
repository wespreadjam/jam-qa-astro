Most late invoices are forgotten, not disputed. A clear, friendly sequence fixes the majority of them.

## The three-step sequence
1. **Day 1 after due:** a short note with the payment link.
2. **Day 7:** restate the amount and ask if anything is blocking payment.
3. **Day 21:** mention the late fee in your contract and offer a call.

Tallyhop users who turned on automatic reminders were paid 11 days sooner on average.
